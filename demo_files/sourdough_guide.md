# Master Sourdough Bread Formula

- 450g Bread Flour (12.7% protein)
- 50g Whole Wheat Flour
- 375g Water (75% hydration)
- 100g Active Leaven
- 10g Fine Sea Salt

Process:
1. 1-hour autolyse with flour and water.
2. Mix starter and salt; 4 sets of stretch and folds every 30 minutes.
3. Cold retard overnight in banneton at 4°C.
